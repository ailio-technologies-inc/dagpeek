Using Dagpeek

Dagpeek is open source under the MIT license. It's provided as is, with no warranty, and we aren't liable for what happens from using it. That includes actions you take on your Dagu servers, such as stopping or retrying a run.

What the app sends us

Once a day, the app sends a usage count to Google Analytics: a random ID for your install, the app version and your operating system. It helps us know how many people use Dagpeek. It never includes your servers, DAGs, runs or credentials. It's on unless you turn it off, which you can do when you first open the app or at any time in Settings.

What stays with you

The app connects directly to the Dagu servers you add. We never see that traffic. Passwords and tokens stay in your system's credential store. The app also checks GitHub for updates.
