Dagpeek

See what Dagu is doing without opening Dagu

A free menu bar and tray app for Mac and Windows. What's running, what's queued, what failed, and how far each run has got.

A number you can trust

Progress comes from the steps Dagu reports. Time left is the typical length of each remaining step over that DAG's last 10 successful runs. With fewer than 3 runs to go on, Dagpeek shows no estimate at all.

If it can't reach your server it says so. It never shows "0 running" when the truth is that it doesn't know.

It stays out of the way

Failures since you last looked sit at the top until you dismiss them. Hide the noisy DAGs by name or tag, and they stop notifying you too. Choose whether you hear about failures, finished runs, or both.

Nothing leaves your machine

Dagpeek talks to the servers you add and to its update feed, and nothing else. There is no account, no analytics and no telemetry. Passwords and tokens stay in your Mac's Keychain or Windows Credential Manager.

Also in your terminal

The same servers, from the dagpeek command.
