See what Dagu is doing without opening Dagu

A free menu bar app for Mac. What's running, what's queued, what failed, and how far each run has got.

Download for Mac

Free and open source. macOS 13 or later, on Apple silicon and Intel. Windows is on the way.

How far along, in steps and in minutes

Open any run to see each step, its status and how long it took. Time left is the typical length of the remaining steps over that DAG's last 10 successful runs.

With fewer than 3 runs to go on, Dagpeek shows no estimate at all.

Failures wait until you've seen them

A run that failed at 3am is still at the top of the list when you sit down. Click it to open the run in Dagu, then dismiss it when you're done.

Hide the DAGs that never stop

Hide health checks and other chatter by name or tag, and they stop notifying you too. Choose whether you hear about failures, finished runs, or both.

It says when it doesn't know

If your server can't be reached, the icon breaks and the popover tells you. Dagpeek never shows "0 running" when the truth is that it can't tell.

Nothing leaves your machine

Dagpeek talks to the servers you add and to its update feed, and nothing else. There is no account, no analytics and no telemetry.

Passwords and tokens stay in your Mac's Keychain. It only reads from Dagu, so it can't start or stop anything.

Install it

With Homebrew

brew install --cask ailio-technologies-inc/tap/dagpeek

Or from the terminal, with the same servers as the app

$ dagpeek status
2 running, 1 queued, 1 failed in the last day
  deploy-web: 4 of 6 steps, on run-migrations, about 70s left
  load-warehouse: 1 of 5 steps, on extract
