Dagpeek shows what your Dagu server is running, what's queued and what failed, without opening the web UI.

How far each run has got

Step counts, the current step, and time left based on that DAG's recent runs. If there isn't enough history for a fair estimate, it doesn't show one.

Failures grouped by DAG

A job that fails every 15 minutes is one line, with a count. It stays there until you dismiss it.

Hide what you don't need

Hide DAGs by name or tag, and they stop notifying you too. Choose what the menu bar shows, from just the icon to a full summary.

Clear when it's disconnected

If it can't reach your server, the icon changes and Dagpeek tells you. It won't show zero runs.

Private and read-only

It only talks to your servers and its update feed. No account or analytics. Passwords and tokens stay in your Keychain, and it can't start or stop anything.

A command too

dagpeek status, runs and watch use the same servers as the app.

Install with Homebrew

brew install --cask ailio-technologies-inc/tap/dagpeek
