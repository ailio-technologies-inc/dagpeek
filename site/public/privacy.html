Dagpeek

Privacy

Ailio Technologies Inc. ("we") makes Dagpeek, a free app, and runs dagpeek.com. This notice covers both. There is no account, and we don't ask for your name or email.

The app

The app connects directly to the Dagu servers you add. That traffic goes between your computer and your servers, and we never see it. If a server needs a sign-in, the password or token is kept in your system's credential store, not in a file we can read. The app also checks GitHub for updates, and GitHub can see the request, like your IP address.

The daily count

Once a day, the app sends one usage count to Google Analytics: a random ID made for your install, the app version and your operating system. Google also receives your IP address with the request, as any server does. We use the count to know how many people use Dagpeek. It never includes your servers, DAGs, runs or credentials. It is on unless you turn it off, which you can do when you first open the app or at any time in Settings.

This website

dagpeek.com uses Google Analytics to count visits: the pages opened, the type of device and an approximate location. Outside Quebec, it runs with cookies from your first visit, and you can say No in the cookie notice at any time. In Quebec, nothing that writes to your device runs until you choose Allow. We store your answer in a cookie called dagpeek_consent, which the site needs to remember it. To change your choice, use the "Change your choice" link at the bottom of the home page. Choosing No removes the Google Analytics cookies. We don't show ads, and we don't use the data for advertising.

Who handles it

Cloudflare hosts dagpeek.com. Google provides Google Analytics for the website and the daily count. GitHub hosts the downloads and the update feed. We don't sell or rent information. We may disclose it where the law requires. Google and GitHub may process information outside Canada, including in the United States, where local laws apply.

How long we keep it

Analytics data stays in Google Analytics under the retention settings we choose there. We keep nothing else about you.

Your rights

You can ask what we hold about you, ask us to correct or delete it, and withdraw your consent. Because the daily count uses a random ID and not your name, we may not be able to tell which data is yours. Email hello@ailio.ca and we'll reply within 30 days. If you aren't satisfied, you can complain to the Office of the Privacy Commissioner of Canada, or in Quebec to the Commission d'accès à l'information.

Contact

The person responsible for protecting personal information at Ailio Technologies Inc. can be reached at hello@ailio.ca. If this notice changes, we'll update this page and its date.
